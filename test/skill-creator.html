A throwaway HTML test page for checking whether skill values feel sensible against the rules in skills.md, plus type and ability pairings from the rest of the docs.

Identity

Builder Stages

Stage Payments

Stages not paid by ACC or slower ATT are paid by SP cost. Each ACC-paid stage applies a 15 ACC penalty. Each ATT-paid stage increases attack time by 20.

Damage Split

Per docs, ATR 100% is fully Physical and ATR 0% is fully Special.

Assumptions

Base ATP, ACC, ATT, and SP come from tier defaults. ATP stages add tier ATP. ACC boost stages add 10 ACC. Faster ATT stages reduce attack time by 20 and can be paid by SP cost or ACC penalties. ATP stages, ACC boost stages, and abilities can each be paid by SP cost, ACC penalties, or slower attack time. SKL bonuses use 0.04 x SKL for ATP/ACC and reduce SP by up to 50%.
