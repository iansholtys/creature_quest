# Companion Types

| Type     | Common Themes      | Common Abilities | Description                                                                                           |
| -------- | ------------------ | ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Oceanic  | Water & Ice        | Freeze           | Creatures of sea and frost, wielding the power of flowing waters and biting cold to overwhelm foes.    |
| Volcanic | Fire & Rock        | Burn             | Born from flame and stone, these creatures scorch the battlefield with intense heat and molten fury.   |
| Earthen  | Grass & Ground     | Constrict        | Rooted in nature and soil, they use vines, roots, and the earth itself to trap and drain opponents.    |
| Aerial   | Flying & Electric  | Paralyze         | Swift masters of the sky, striking from above with lightning-fast attacks that numb their prey.        |
| Crawly   | Bug & Poison       | Poison           | Lowly but relentless, these creatures lurk in shadows and infest battlefields with venom and toxins.   |
| Spooky   | Ghost & Dark       | Sleep            | Haunting beings from beyond, draining life and lulling enemies into slumber with eerie powers.         |
| Mental   | Psychic & Fairy    | Confuse          | Ethereal and enchanting, they bend the minds of opponents with psychic force and mystical charm.       |
| Physical | Normal & Fighting  | Stun             | Raw power incarnate, relying on brute strength and martial prowess to crush anything in their path.     |
