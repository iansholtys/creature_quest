# Training
Unlike in other games, Creature Quest does not feature an experience and leveling system like the others do. Instead, creatures improve by actually doing things!

## Training in Combat


##### Skill Use in Combat
Each skill can get stronger as it's used in combat by keeping track of the SKL. This is a 1:1 tracking mechanism.


## Daily Training Regiment
Every night (as well as every day during downtime) your companion(s) grow a little stronger through training. 



## Dojos
Companions that no longer fit in your party can continue to train while you're away if you've got slots in friendly dojos that they can stay at while you travel the world! Although they don't get the normal bonuses for Training in Combat, they can still max out their stats eventually through their Daily training.
